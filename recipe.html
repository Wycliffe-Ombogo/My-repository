Rice Beef Stir Fry

25 mins Serves 4 420 cal/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably day-old)
  • 1 cup thinly sliced beef (sirloin or ribeye)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoons oyster sauce (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • Red pepper flakes (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
  2. Add onions and garlic, stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add beef slices and cook until browned (2-3 minutes).
  4. Toss in peas and carrots, stir-fry for 2 more minutes.
  5. Add cooked rice, breaking up any clumps.
  6. Pour soy sauce and oyster sauce over the rice mixture.
  7. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until everything is evenly coated and heated through.
  8. Season with red pepper flakes if desired.

Nutrition Facts

Nutrient Per Serving
Calories 420
Protein 24g
Carbohydrates 45g
Fat 14g
Fiber 3g

Chef's Tips

  • For crispier beef, pat dry with paper towels before cooking.
  • Add a scrambled egg for extra protein.
  • Substitute brown rice for a healthier option.
