Persian Rice with Golden Crust (Tahdig)

1 hour Serves 6 380 cal/serving

About Tahdig

The golden, crispy crust at the bottom of the pot is the most prized part of Persian rice. This recipe guarantees perfect tahdig every time!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 3 quarts water
  • 3 tablespoons kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 russet potato, sliced into ¼-inch rounds
  • 1 pinch ground cumin
  • 3 tablespoons butter, sliced thin
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 1½ tablespoons hot water
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Prep Rice: Rinse rice until water runs clear. Soak in warm water for 30 mins, then drain.
  2. Parboil: Bring 3 quarts water and salt to boil. Add rice, cook exactly 7 mins. Drain and rinse with cool water.
  3. Prepare Tahdig: Heat oil in non-stick pot over medium. Arrange potato slices in single layer. Sprinkle with cumin.
  4. Layer Rice: Gently spoon parboiled rice over potatoes. Create steam holes with handle of wooden spoon.
  5. Cook: Place butter slices on rice. Cover with clean kitchen towel, then lid. Cook on low heat 45 mins.
  6. Saffron: Crush saffron with mortar/pestle. Dissolve in 1½ tbsp hot water. Mix with 1 cup rice to color yellow.
  7. Serve: Invert pot onto platter to reveal golden crust. Top with saffron rice and garnish with parsley.

Pro Tips

  • For dramatic presentation, bring the whole pot to the table before flipping.
  • Alternative tahdig: Use lavash bread instead of potatoes.
  • Add barberries (zereshk) for authentic Persian flavor.
