🌟 About This Project
Welcome to my personal culinary portfolio! This website showcases my passion for cooking, recipe development, and the fascinating intersection of food science and biotechnology. As a 19-year-old biotechnology student at JKUAT, I've created this platform to share my favorite recipes, cooking techniques, and nutritional insights.

🍳 Key Features
Recipe Collection: Beautifully presented recipes with detailed instructions

Nutritional Information: Complete nutrition facts for health-conscious cooking

Professional Layout: Responsive design that works on all devices

Interactive Elements: Hover effects and visual enhancements

Personal Branding: Showcases my unique culinary perspective
